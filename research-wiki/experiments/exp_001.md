# Setup

Train LFQ VAE with enhanced losses on sign language data (CSL-Daily / How2Sign / PHOENIX). Compare reconstruction quality vs. original SOKE VQ.

## Configs

`configs/vq/re128_lfq4.yaml`, `configs/vq/hand256_lfq4.yaml`, `configs/vq/hand256_lfq5.yaml`

## Results

LFQ + richer losses (velocity, FK, accel, contact) produces more humanly-recognizable reconstructed sign sequences. Qualitative improvement is clear.

## Verdict

**Supports claim:C1** — LFQ with enhanced reconstruction losses improves reconstruction quality.
**Supports partial failure of idea:001 hypothesis** — better reconstruction did not transfer to downstream LM.
